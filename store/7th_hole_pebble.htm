"7th Hole at Pebble Beach"

The iconic 7th hole at Pebble Beach Golf Links is a short par 3 perched above the Pacific — one of the most photographed and revered holes in golf. Michael P. Mastropolo captures its dramatic coastal setting with radiant light, lush greens, and the endless blue of the ocean beyond.
