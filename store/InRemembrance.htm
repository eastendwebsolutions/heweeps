 "In Remembrance"

This extraordinary image entitled "In Remembrance", depicting the horror, honor and hope that surrounds the tragic, historic, 9 11 attack on our freedom , is now being offered as a "state of the art" giclee canvas. This highly sophisticated fine art giclee process is a superior method of reproduction, resulting in the highest quality fine art prints in the market today. The giclee canvas imparts the feel and look of the original painting without compromising it's rich colors and fine detail .This innovative process is more expensive than the traditional reproduction process, but the final result is worthed.  The giclee canvas is a replica of the original painting , sold at a fraction of it's cost.  "In Remembrance" is being offered in a variety of sizes, framed and unframed.
