"12th hole at Augusta "

The famous and majestic12th hole at Augusta National has never been painted with such radiant light, magnificent color and precise detail until now. This highly challenging hole is the " center piece " of Amen Corner . Named "Golden Bell," it is one of the most famous and respected holes in the game , by the best players on this planet .The Masters Tournament is played there every year showcasing the top pros in golf. Although the hole is the shortest par 3 at 155yds, it is considered the most difficult to conquer. The green is like a well guarded castle surrounded by Rae’s Creek and 3 strategically placed bunkers. The notorious winds add a another dimension of difficulty to "Golden Bell". The center of the green is less than 10 yards deep requiring pin point accuracy and skill to successfully place a shot in good position. The 12th hole at Augusta is one of incredible, physical beauty and strong spiritual presence, evident when each player has to cross The Ben Hogan Bridge to finally step on the green. This image painted by Long Island artist Michael P. Mastropolo is now being offered in a Giclee Limited Edition of various sizes, framed and unframed. Each image is signed and numbered by the artist.
